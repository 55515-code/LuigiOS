# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Welcome people across experience levels
and backgrounds. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or credential requests.

Protect privacy. Do not publish another person's identifying information,
network address, logs, account data, private keys, or proprietary source.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers.
