# Credits

LuigiOS builds on work from CachyOS, Arch Linux, System76's COSMIC project,
systemd, Btrfs, Snapper, The Update Framework, BitTorrent Enhancement Proposals,
Sigstore, SLSA, SPDX, OCI, Transmission, Syncthing, Podman, Papirus, and the
broader free-software community.

Project-owned artwork and configuration provenance is recorded under `branding/`.
Package-level licensing remains available through pacman metadata and installed
license directories.
